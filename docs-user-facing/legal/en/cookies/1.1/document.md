# Cookie Policy

This Policy describes cookies used by the hosted **ai_stp** service, operated by
[ai-engineers-guild](https://github.com/ai-engineers-guild), identified by its
public GitHub organization. Contact:
[admin@aiguild.space](mailto:admin@aiguild.space).

## Necessary cookies

These cookies provide and secure the service and are always on:

- a secure, HTTP-only session cookie authenticates the signed-in browser;
- a CSRF cookie and matching request header protect state-changing requests;
- a secure, HTTP-only browser-device cookie associates the browser with the
  registered device used for account security;
- the `ai_stp_consent` cookie remembers your cookie choices for 180 days.

The session, CSRF and device cookies normally expire within 14 days and may end
earlier when you log out, revoke a device, clear browser storage, or the server
revokes the session. Blocking them prevents browser sign-in or protected account
actions from working correctly.

## Optional analytics cookies

The public pages can measure their use with Google Analytics (Google) and Yandex
Metrica (Yandex). Their scripts and cookies stay off until you allow analytics in
**Cookie settings**. Once allowed, Google Analytics may set cookies such as
`_ga` and `_ga_<ID>`, and Yandex Metrica may set cookies such as `_ym_uid`,
`_ym_d` and `_ym_isad`. The providers set their own lifetimes, up to about two
years. They receive the data described in the Privacy Policy under their own
terms.

You can withdraw consent at any time in **Cookie settings**. The service then
stops loading the analytics scripts and deletes the analytics cookies it can
reach from the site.

## No advertising cookies

The service does not use advertising cookies or build advertising profiles.

Browser settings can delete or block cookies. Privacy questions may be sent to
[admin@aiguild.space](mailto:admin@aiguild.space).
