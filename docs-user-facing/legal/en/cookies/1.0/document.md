# Cookie Policy

This Policy describes cookies used by the hosted **ai_stp** service, operated by
[ai-engineers-guild](https://github.com/ai-engineers-guild), identified by its
public GitHub organization. Contact:
[admin@aiguild.space](mailto:admin@aiguild.space).

## Cookies in use

ai_stp uses only cookies that are necessary to provide and secure the service:

- a secure, HTTP-only session cookie authenticates the signed-in browser;
- a CSRF cookie and matching request header protect state-changing requests;
- a secure, HTTP-only browser-device cookie associates the browser with the
  registered device used for account security.

These cookies normally expire within 14 days and may end earlier when you log
out, revoke a device, clear browser storage, or the server revokes the session.
Blocking them prevents browser sign-in or protected account actions from
working correctly.

## No advertising or optional analytics cookies

The service does not use advertising cookies, cross-site behavioural tracking,
or optional analytics cookies. For that reason, it does not display a cosmetic
cookie-consent banner. If optional cookies are introduced, they will remain off
until a separate opt-in is obtained where required, and this document will
receive a new version.

Browser settings can delete or block cookies. Privacy questions may be sent to
[admin@aiguild.space](mailto:admin@aiguild.space).
