# Brand

The product is ai-stp; UI wordmarks may use `ai_stp`. The primary product is the
Rust CLI for AI harness configurations, with web account/catalog and desktop clients.

Current assets live in `apps/web/public/brand`; icons and colors come from the theme.
Use the existing mark with its transparent background and preserve its proportions.
IBM Plex Sans (`plexSans`) and IBM Plex Mono (`plexMono`) are the self-hosted product typefaces.
Do not invent a separate logo, token palette or font stack for a feature.

For interface composition, follow [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) and the registered web kit.
